Membership

Nobody applies cold.

You arrive because someone vouched for you. You stay because the circle said yes.

I

A member puts your name forward.

Every new member is brought in by an existing one.

II

The circle decides.

Members of the circle you're joining review the request, then accept or pass.

III

You're in, with four invitations.

A member number, a card, and four invitations of your own.

Every member can see who brought them in. The lineage is the credential.

HOUSE RULES

  1. Real names, real faces.

    Everyone here is who they say they are. No handles.

  2. The circle stays in the circle.

    What's said at the table isn't posted, screenshotted or repeated.

  3. Recommend as if your name is on it.

    Because it is. Only send members where you'd send a friend.

  4. An RSVP is a promise.

    Someone else wanted your seat. Show up, or release it early.

  5. Invite with care.

    Bring people who make the room better. Your name travels with them.
